Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2, 2026 · Collateify · Pandobal Limited

Overview

Collateify is provided by Pandobal Limited (Hong Kong). This Privacy Policy explains how the Collateify app handles information when you use it on your device.

Collateify organizes your photos on this device. Photos are not uploaded to our servers.

Permissions we use

Photos: Access is used only to read and organize your photo library on your device.

Notifications: Used to remind you about 2 days before a free trial ends, so you can avoid an unexpected renewal.

Location data in photos: The photo info screen may show a place name derived from location data already stored in a photo’s metadata. We do not track your live location.

Subscriptions & payments

Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple or Google through your store account. We do not collect or store your payment card details.

Your choices

You can turn off Photos or Notifications access in system settings at any time. You can cancel subscription renewal in system subscription settings (for example, iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions).
