Common questions

How do I organize photos?

Open Collateify, pick a time period (month or week), then swipe right to keep or left to delete. You can switch to left-hand mode in Settings.

Does Collateify upload my photos?

No. Photos stay on your device. We do not upload your photo library to our servers.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. On iPhone: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Collateify. You can cancel anytime at least 24 hours before renewal.

I found a bug or need more help

Email hello@pandobal.com with your device model, iOS version, and a short description. Screenshots help us respond faster.
